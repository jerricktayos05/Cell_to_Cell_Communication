## Biological Question

How do adrenocortical cells communicate with skeletal muscle cells to help the body respond to stress and regulate energy metabolism?

## Chosen Sender Cell

**Sender cell:** Adrenocortical cell 

**Biological context:** Adrenocortical cells are endocrine cells located in the adrenal cortex. Cells in the zona fasciculata produce cortisol, a steroid hormone that is released into the bloodstream and acts on distant target cells, including skeletal muscle cells.

## Signaling Molecule

**Candidate signaling molecule:** Cortisol

**Synthesis-related gene:** CYP11B1

**Signaling type:** Endocrine

Cortisol is a steroid hormone synthesized in the zona fasciculata of the adrenal cortex. It travels through the bloodstream and can enter skeletal muscle cells, where it binds to the intracellular glucocorticoid receptor (NR3C1). This signaling pathway influences gene expression involved in stress responses and energy metabolism.

### 1. What sender cell did you choose, and in what tissue or biological context does it act?

The sender cell is a **zona fasciculata cell of the adrenal cortex**, located in the outer region of the adrenal gland. These cells produce cortisol, a steroid hormone involved in the body's response to stress and the regulation of energy metabolism.

### 2. What signaling molecule did you identify, and what evidence supports its production or presentation by the sender cell?

The signaling molecule is **cortisol**. Evidence from the Human Protein Atlas shows that CYP11B1, a gene encoding an enzyme involved in cortisol synthesis, is expressed in adrenal cortex cells, including cells of the zona fasciculata. This supports the adrenal cortex as the source of cortisol, although CYP11B1 expression alone does not directly measure cortisol release.

### 3. What receptor receives the signal, and which receiver cell did you select?

The receptor is the **glucocorticoid receptor (GR), encoded by NR3C1**, and the receiver cell is a skeletal muscle cell. Because cortisol is a steroid hormone, it can cross the cell membrane and bind to the intracellular glucocorticoid receptor. The activated receptor can then influence gene expression.

### 4. What type of cell-to-cell signaling is represented?

The model represents **endocrine signaling**. Cortisol is released by cells in the adrenal cortex and transported through the bloodstream to distant target cells, including skeletal muscle cells. This distinguishes endocrine signaling from paracrine signaling, which acts mainly on nearby cells.
